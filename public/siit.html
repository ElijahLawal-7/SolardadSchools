Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Who can enroll in the SIIT program? ▼

The program is open to students, graduates, and young people ready to position themselves as leaders in the digital economy. It's designed not to interfere with regular academic programs.

How can I apply ? ▼

The first step in the application procedures is to fill out the online interest form. The visit Solardad College, and purchase the formal application form at N5,000.
Fill the form and submit, after which you will be given the official acceptance letter and accepted to commence the Program. Note that you are not mandated to pay the program cost before you begin.

What are the payment terms? ▼

Fees are paid per term. Web Development is ₦35,000 per term, Software Engineering and Cybersecurity are ₦40,000 per term, and Computer Applications is ₦28,000 per term.

Will I receive a certificate after completion? ▼

Yes! All graduates receive recognized professional certifications that are valuable in the job market both locally and internationally.

What are the class schedules? ▼

Classes are scheduled flexibly to accommodate students' regular school activities. Specific schedules will be provided upon enrollment.

Do I need prior computer knowledge? ▼

Basic computer literacy is helpful but not required for the Computer Applications course. Other courses are designed for beginners and progressively build advanced skills.

Can I work while learning these skills? ▼

Absolutely! Many of our students start freelancing or taking on small projects even before completing their courses. We provide guidance on how to monetize your skills early.

Where is the training held? ▼

All training takes place at Solardad College, No. 1 Ikuehi Close, G.R.A, Okene, in our modern computer laboratories.
