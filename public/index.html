Our Mission

Since inception, we have empahsized our aim, which is to inculcate the spirit of self-reliance and innate desire for learning as a continuous process and decent social behavior among the students. Wisdom and modernity has been the key word whose all-round personality development echoes in the integration of three: good health, good moral character and a keen sense of observation

Our Schools

Solardad College - Secondary School

At Solardad College, we cultivate the leaders of tomorrow by providing a dynamic and holistic learning experience. Our secondary school curriculum is designed to challenge students academically while fostering creativity, critical thinking, and leadership skills. With state-of-the-art facilities, highly qualified educators, and a strong emphasis on discipline and moral values, we ensure that every student is prepared for higher education and beyond.

Primary School

A strong foundation in education is the key to lifelong success, and at Solardad Primary School, we provide just that. Our nurturing environment encourages curiosity, innovation, and confidence in young learners. Through interactive and engaging teaching methods, we help students develop a love for learning while instilling essential life skills. Our dedicated teachers ensure that each child receives personalized attention, laying the groundwork for academic excellence and social development.

Nursery School

The early years of a child's life are crucial for cognitive and emotional development. At Solardad Nursery School, we offer a warm and stimulating environment where little ones can explore, discover, and grow. Our play-based learning approach fosters creativity, communication, and problem-solving skills, ensuring that children develop a solid foundation for future learning. With caring educators and a safe, vibrant setting, we make the first steps of education joyful and exciting for every child.
